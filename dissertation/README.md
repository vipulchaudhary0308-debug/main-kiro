## Headline Findings

- **Total** vocational interest score of boys and girls is **almost equal** (t = 0.92, NS).
- **Out of 9 areas, 8 show significant gender differences (p < .01).** Only the **Commercial** area is gender-neutral (t = 1.65, NS).
- **Boys** are higher on **Technical (t = 8.54), Scientific (t = 4.35), Executive (t = 2.81) and Agriculture (t = 6.49)**.
- **Girls** are higher on **Household (t = 8.25), Artistic (t = 5.48), Literary (t = 3.85) and Social (t = 3.64)**.
- The **biggest gender gaps** are on the **Technical** (boys higher) and **Household** (girls higher) areas.
- Class IX vs Class X — **no significant difference** (t = 1.48, NS); vocational interest is already crystallised by class IX.
- Boys' first-preference area is in the **Executive-Scientific-Technical** trio (80%); girls' first-preference area is in the **Household-Literary-Artistic-Social** quartet (87.5%).

All four null hypotheses (H0₁ on totals, H0₂ on areas, H0₃ on class, H0₄ on rank-order) are largely **rejected**, except where indicated above.
