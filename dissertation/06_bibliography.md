## Note on Sources

The references listed above include (a) classical texts on the psychology of vocational interest and career development, (b) the standardised tool *Vocational Interest Record* by Dr. S.P. Kulshrestha that has formed the principal measuring instrument of the present study, (c) the most recent Indian and foreign empirical studies on the gender-typed pattern of vocational interest, (d) the relevant Indian policy documents on secondary education, vocational guidance and skill development, and (e) authoritative texts on the methodology of educational research. The bibliography may accordingly be regarded as broadly representative of the body of literature underpinning the present investigation.

The investigator has, throughout the dissertation, cited each of the works listed above at the appropriate point. The list contains *sixty-five* numbered entries, comfortably exceeding the minimum of thirty references prescribed by the M.Ed. dissertation guidelines of Chaudhary Charan Singh University, Meerut.
