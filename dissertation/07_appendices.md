## APPENDIX F: SAMPLE CALCULATIONS (STEP-BY-STEP)

### F.1 Calculation of Mean and SD — Rural Boys, Technical Interest

**Scores:** 30, 32, 28, 34, 26, 32, 28, 30, 32, 28, 30, 32, 26, 34, 30

**Step 1: Sum of Scores (ΣX)**
ΣX = 30+32+28+34+26+32+28+30+32+28+30+32+26+34+30 = **452**

**Step 2: Mean (M)**
M = ΣX / N = 452 / 15 = **30.13**

**Step 3: Sum of Squared Scores (ΣX²)**
ΣX² = 900+1024+784+1156+676+1024+784+900+1024+784+900+1024+676+1156+900 = **13,712**

**Step 4: Standard Deviation (SD)**
SD = √[(ΣX²/N) - M²]
= √[(13712/15) - (30.13)²]
= √[914.13 - 907.82]
= √6.31
= **2.51**

*Note: The slight discrepancy between this manually computed value (2.51) and the precise computed value (2.47) is due to rounding in the mean (30.13 is rounded from 30.1333...). The precisely calculated SD using the exact mean is 2.47.*

---

### F.2 Sample t-test Calculation — Social Interest: Boys vs. Girls

**Given:**
- M₁ (Boys) = 18.07; M₂ (Girls) = 29.07
- SD₁ = 2.94; SD₂ = 1.84
- N₁ = N₂ = 30; df = 58

**Step 1: SE of Difference**
SE = √[(SD₁²/N₁) + (SD₂²/N₂)]
= √[(2.94²/30) + (1.84²/30)]
= √[(8.6436/30) + (3.3856/30)]
= √[0.28812 + 0.11285]
= √0.40097
= **0.6332**

**Step 2: t-value**
t = (M₁ - M₂) / SE
= (18.07 - 29.07) / 0.6332
= -11.00 / 0.6332
= **-17.37** (|t| = 17.37)

**Step 3: Decision**
Critical t at df=58, α=0.05 (two-tailed) = 2.002
Since |t| = 17.37 > 2.002, the null hypothesis is **REJECTED**.
